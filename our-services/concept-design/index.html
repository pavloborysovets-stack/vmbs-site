ENDEPL

Concept design & design development

Before you spend on design and tendering, we check whether the project can work: the site, the permit route, the scope and a realistic budget range.

What we do

  • Review your brief, site and permit situation: we check the status of the local plan, MPZP and WZ for the plot.
  • Define the scope a manufacturer can actually price: modules or panels, level of prefabrication, interfaces with the site.
  • Build a realistic cost corridor from our experience with factories in Poland and the Baltics.
  • Identify risks early: access and transport, foundations, utilities, permit steps.

What you receive

A short written Go/No-Go memo: what is feasible, what is not, what must be clarified, and the recommended next step.

Who it is for

Developers, investors and intermediaries with a project or a site who want a clear decision before committing to design.

Questions clients ask

How long does concept design & design development take?

It is project-specific. As a guide, concept design & design development takes 1–2 months.

What do I receive at the end of concept design & design development?

A volumetric massing model with MMC specifics, suitable for the further design stages, with the volumetric and prefab logic already incorporated.

What do you need from me to start?

The site and its location (drawings, address, plot boundaries), the project brief and project programme if available, your written vision of the project if available, and any other relevant materials.
