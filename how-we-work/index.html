From inquiry to handover in 7 steps

Each step has a clear result, so you always know what has been decided and what comes next.

  1. Start

    Inquiry

    You send your project through the form: country and site, type of building, approximate area, target schedule and permit status. We reply by e-mail within one working day.

    You get: A reply and next steps

  2. Stages 0–2

    Concept design & design development

    We assess the site, the scope and the cost corridor of your modular or prefab project.

    You get: A Go/No-Go decision

  3. Stage 3

    Manufacturer selection

    We shortlist modular and prefab manufacturers in Poland and the Baltics, prepare the RFQ package and compare the offers. You choose.

    You get: RFQ package and comparison of offers

  4. Stage 3

    Contracting

    You sign directly with the manufacturer and the contractors. We are on your side, not a party to their contracts.

    You get: Contracts in your name

  5. Stage 4

    Preparation

    We coordinate technical and production design data, permits through the local architect, and site readiness.

    You get: A shared schedule for all parties

  6. Stage 5

    Delivery and installation

    We coordinate manufacturing, transport, installation on site and acceptances.

    You get: Coordinated delivery and acceptances

  7. Stage 6

    Handover

    We prepare the handover of the finished building to you.

    You get: A completed building handed over

Clear scope

  • We are not the architect or structural engineer of record.
  • We are not the general contractor.
  • We coordinate schedules. We do not promise fixed dates, because delivery and installation depend on parties outside our control.

Questions clients ask

Who signs the contract with the manufacturer?

You do. You sign directly with the manufacturer and the contractors. VMBS.TECH works on your side as consultant and coordinator.

Are you the architect or the general contractor?

No. We are not the architect or structural engineer of record and not the general contractor. In every country we work through a local architect who is responsible for the design documentation.

Which project stages do you cover?

We deliver stages 0–3 (strategy and feasibility, brief and Go/No-Go, concept design, coordination and manufacturer choice) and coordinate stages 4–6 (technical and production design, manufacturing and installation, handover) on your side. Stage 7, use and warranty, is outside our scope.

Which manufacturers do you work with?

We know modular and timber-frame factories in Poland and the Baltic states — Estonia, Latvia and Lithuania — and work with partner timber-frame factories.

Do you guarantee delivery dates?

We coordinate schedules. We do not promise fixed dates, because delivery and installation depend on parties outside our control.

How quickly do you reply?

We reply by e-mail within one working day.

Have a site or a project?

Tell us the basics. We reply by e-mail within one working day.
