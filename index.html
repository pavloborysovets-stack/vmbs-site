How we work

Three parties, one shared schedule. We keep the client, the manufacturer and the site in step.

  1. Inquiry

    You send your project through the form.

  2. Pre-design

    We assess site, scope and cost corridor and give you a Go/No-Go.

  3. Manufacturer selection

    Shortlist, RFQ package, comparison of offers. You choose.

  4. Contracting

    You sign with the manufacturer and the contractors.

  5. Preparation

    We coordinate design data, permits and site readiness.

  6. Delivery and installation

    We coordinate transport, installation and acceptances.

  7. Handover

    We prepare the handover of the finished building.

Clear scope

  • We are not the architect of record and we do not design structures.
  • We are not the general contractor.
  • We coordinate schedules. We do not promise fixed dates, because delivery and installation depend on parties outside our control.

ARC — Automated Robotic Complexes

A project we are developing on automation and robotisation of production and assembly sites, off-site and on-site.

Instead of one universal system, a set of specialised automated cells is brought to the production or construction site, installed in fenced zones with defined material hand-over points, and then works autonomously, with no people inside the zones.
