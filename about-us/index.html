About us

Who we are

VMBS.TECH, based in Tczew (Poland) and Riga (Latvia), is today a collaboration of professionals in volumetric modular construction. We work with developers, investors and intermediaries who want to bring a modular or prefab house project to a manufacturer with a realistic concept. We are a remote team working effectively together on projects worldwide.

Background

  • About 20 years of experience in construction and real estate development.
  • Market experience in Poland, Germany, the Netherlands and Norway.
  • Project management and special projects at modular house manufacturers.

How we work

  • We are consultants and coordinators. We are not the architect of record and not the general contractor.
  • We know factories in Poland and the Baltics and work with partner timber-frame factories. We have studied the manufacturer market of Poland and all the Baltic states: Estonia, Latvia and Lithuania.
  • In every country we work through a local architect who is responsible for the design documentation.

Our aim

We want to help create good living environments for people.
