MMC / DfMA design & development

Design for Manufacture and Assembly: we design buildings and products so that a factory can make them and a site can assemble them.

What we do

  • MMC architectural design
  • Product development
  • Project management and digital transformation
  • BIM design and modeling
  • Customer development and market research
  • Modularization and optimization of construction projects
  • Infographics and visualization
  • Systematization and research

What you receive

Design documentation and models prepared for off-site production, a modularized project with optimized logistics and assembly, and the research behind it.

Who it is for

Developers, manufacturers and ConTech companies launching or scaling prefab and modular products.
