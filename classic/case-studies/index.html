Case studies

Our projects are made in varying degrees of prefabrication: from prefabricated structures to fully factory-produced volumetric modular buildings.

Track record of our team

  • Over our careers, members of our team have taken part in 60+ projects with a total of 5,000+ m² across 7 countries.
  • Germany: more than 20 individual houses to a high energy standard, built together with partner timber-frame factories.
  • Germany: a hotel – design, production, installation and service.
  • Germany: a multi-apartment building.
  • Germany: a three-storey serial modular residential building; airtightness test n50 = 0.52.
  • Netherlands: four private year-round houses of about 70 m², each made of two modules, and a seven-module building with 3 apartments for a care home.

Certification and first phase

  • Ü-Zeichen certification for timber house construction is in place.
  • Bremen: the first phase is built, a three-storey residential house within a townhouse development.
