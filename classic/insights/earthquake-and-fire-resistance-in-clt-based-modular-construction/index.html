Earthquake and Fire Resistance in CLT-based Modular Construction

By Pavlo Borysovets · 4 October 2024 · 2 min read

Earthquake Resistance: Modular Construction with Superior Safety

When it comes to earthquake resilience, modular construction, especially when utilizing Cross-Laminated Timber (CLT), outperforms traditional building methods like brick or concrete. At our modular building company, we've taken earthquake resistance to the next level, ensuring that each modular house or modular home we design provides the highest level of safety.

Seismic studies by Italian and Japanese researchers demonstrated that a full-sized seven-story modular home made from CLT successfully withstood a 6.9 magnitude earthquake. For comparison, this is the same force that hit Kobe, Japan, in 1995, causing the collapse of over 50,000 buildings. But thanks to the flexibility and layered structure of CLT, our modular structures adapt to seismic vibrations rather than resist them, guaranteeing superior safety during earthquakes.

As a modular house manufacturer committed to safety, we implement these CLT technologies across our projects, ensuring that all our volumetric buildings and modular homes are resilient against natural disasters. The modular approach also allows for easy transport and rapid assembly in earthquake-prone areas, offering a highly effective and practical solution for developers and municipalities.

Fire Resistance: Durable and Safe Modular Homes

Fire safety is another critical factor in the design and construction of modern buildings. Our volumetric building company prioritizes fire resistance in every modular home we manufacture, and the use of CLT in our designs significantly enhances fire safety.

Laboratory tests have shown that CLT can withstand extreme temperatures of over 980°C for more than three hours. During a fire, the outer layers of CLT char, forming an insulating barrier that protects the structural integrity of the deeper layers. This makes modular construction using CLT far more fire-resistant than traditional materials like steel or concrete, which lose their strength rapidly in high heat.

Because of this, our modular houses are designed to ensure not only fire protection but also safe evacuation. The charring process slows down the combustion, allowing people more time to escape, which is essential for buildings in densely populated urban environments or multi-family residential projects.

Which volumetric modular building company should you choose?

With years of experience in modular construction, we stand out as a modular house manufacturer that delivers safety, speed, and sustainability. Our use of CLT offers unmatched advantages in both earthquake and fire resistance. By choosing our volumetric building company, you're investing in:

  1. Safety: earthquake and fire-resistant designs based on CLT technology.

  4. Customization: our modular houses and buildings can be tailored to meet your specific needs, whether for residential, commercial, or public infrastructure.
